# Contact

We are a family, not a call centre — but we answer.

## Write to us

**hive@mountain-breath.example** — orders, questions, wholesale, or just to
tell us which jar you liked. We reply within a day, usually faster; in
July, when the linden keeps us in the meadow, sometimes a little slower.

## Call or message

**+374 91 00 00 00** — Armenian, Russian and English spoken. Messages
(WhatsApp/Telegram) are easiest, since veils and phones mix badly.

## Visit

The apiary sits above the village at the end of a gravel road that maps
disagree about. Write first and we will send directions and pick a sunny
morning — the bees are friendlier then, and so is the road.

## The shop with your jar

If something is wrong with an order — a broken jar, a missing item, a
courier mystery — write with your order number and a photo if there is
something to photograph. We fix it first and ask questions later.
