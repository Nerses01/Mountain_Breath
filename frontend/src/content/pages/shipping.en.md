# Shipping

We pack every order within one working day, in paper and straw — no
plastic, because honey travelled fine for a few thousand years without it.

## Where and how much

Within Armenia we ship with a courier, usually next-day in Yerevan and one
to three days elsewhere. The exact fee is shown in your cart before you
pay, in your own currency; orders over the free-shipping threshold ship
free.

Chilled products — royal jelly above all — travel in an insulated box with
a cold pack. The chilled surcharge stays even when standard shipping is
free, because the cold box costs us real money either way. Your first
order's standard delivery is on us.

## When it arrives

If a jar arrives broken, photograph it as it came and write to us — we
replace it, no questions. If nobody is home, the courier calls; you can
also tell us at checkout to leave the parcel with a neighbour.

## Abroad

We are a small family shop and do not ship abroad yet. When that changes,
the harvest notes will say it first.
