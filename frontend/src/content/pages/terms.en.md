# Terms of sale

The short, honest version first: you order, we pack, a courier brings it,
and if anything is wrong we make it right. The longer version follows.

## Prices and payment

Prices are shown in the currency you shop in and include VAT. The price at
the moment you place the order is the price — if we change a shelf price
afterwards, your order is not affected. Payment is by card, bank transfer
or cash on delivery (drams only). Bank-transfer orders ship once the
transfer clears.

## Orders and cancellation

You can cancel an order any time before it ships — write to us or call.
Once a parcel is with the courier, it can only be refused at the door,
which returns it to us and we refund the goods.

## Returns

Honey, propolis and everything else edible are food: for safety we can
only take back unopened jars, within 14 days. Beeswax and candles come
back within 14 days in any state that lets us sell them again. Broken or
wrong deliveries are entirely on us — photo, message, replacement.

## The small print

We sell as a registered family enterprise under the laws of the Republic
of Armenia. Disputes go first to a conversation, which has so far always
been enough.
