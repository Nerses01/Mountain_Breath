# What the hive does for you

Every jar and tincture on this shelf earns its place by what it does in the
body. Here is the honest version — what tradition says, what studies
suggest, and where the line is.

## Honey and pollen — steady natural energy

Raw honey is mostly fast sugars, but unfiltered honey keeps its pollen,
enzymes and trace minerals. Pollen granules carry protein — around a
quarter by weight — with all essential amino acids. A spoon in the morning
is food, not medicine, and that is the point.

## Propolis — the hive's own defence

Bees seal their home with propolis because almost nothing survives on it.
Its flavonoids have shown antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory activity in
laboratory studies. We take it as drops in warm water at the first sign of
a scratchy throat.

## Royal jelly — vitality and skin

The food of queens, dense in B-vitamins and unique fatty acids. Small
clinical studies point at skin elasticity and general vigour. It is the
most delicate thing we sell — kept cold from hive to your door.

## Beeswax — balms, creams and candles

Not eaten, but worked: beeswax carries oils, holds scent and burns slow
and clean. Ours is rendered from cappings only, the whitest and freshest
wax a hive makes.

---

*Nothing here is a medicine, and none of it replaces one. If you are
allergic to bee products, pregnant, or treating a condition, talk to your
doctor first — and start any new bee product with a small amount.*
