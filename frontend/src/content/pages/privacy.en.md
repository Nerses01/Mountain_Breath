# Privacy

We keep bees, not dossiers. Here is everything we know about you and why.

## What we store

Your account is an email and a password we never see in the clear — only a
one-way hash. Orders keep the delivery address, phone and items exactly as
they were at purchase, because a receipt must not change afterwards. If
you save addresses or heart products, those live with your account until
you delete them.

## What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, full stop. There are
no advertising trackers on this site. The only cookies are the one that
keeps you signed in and the ones that remember your language and currency.

## Email

We write to you for two reasons only: something about an order of yours,
or — if you asked for the harvest notes AND clicked the confirmation link
— one letter a month about the meadow. Every such letter carries an
unsubscribe link that works forever.

## Your rights

Write to us and we will show you what we store about you, correct it, or
delete your account entirely. Orders we must keep for bookkeeping as the
law requires; everything else goes.
