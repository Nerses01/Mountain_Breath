---
title: The linden weeks
date: 2026-07-18
teaser: Two hundred hives working one week of bloom, and a visitor who lifted her first frame.
---

Everything on this meadow happens slowly except the linden. When the old
trees along the river open, the whole apiary changes sound — a deeper,
busier hum you can hear from the road. The bloom lasts two weeks in a good
year. This was a good year.

We move nothing during the linden weeks; the bees know where the trees
are better than we do. We just add supers and keep out of the way. The
honey from these weeks is the one most of you know us for — amber, slow,
with that cool menthol finish that comes only from linden.

A reader of these notes visited on Tuesday and lifted her first frame,
gloved and veiled and very brave. The bees ignored her completely, which
is the best possible review of the weather.

Jarring starts the first week of August. The harvest notes will say when
the shelf changes.
