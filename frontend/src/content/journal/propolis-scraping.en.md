---
title: Propolis, the unglamorous harvest
date: 2026-08-10
teaser: Scraping the hive's own medicine, and why the best crumbs never see a grinder.
---

Nobody photographs propolis harvesting for a postcard. It is scraping
resin off frames and walls in August heat, and the bees resent every
minute of it. But this sticky brown glue is the hive's own medicine — the
reason a box of forty thousand animals stays cleaner than most kitchens.

We freeze the scrapings so the resin cracks clean instead of smearing,
break them into crumbs on a cold table, and pick out the wax and wood
splinters by hand. There is no shortcut; warm propolis smears and takes
the sweepings with it. The pouches on the shelf right now are June's
scraping. What we scraped this week becomes the batch you will see in
October.

The autumn flowers are opening on the high slope — the dark honey is
coming. One more letter before the season turns.
