---
title: Propolis, the unglamorous harvest
date: 2026-08-10
teaser: Scraping the hive's own medicine, and why the tincture takes six weeks you cannot rush.
---

Nobody photographs propolis harvesting for a postcard. It is scraping
resin off frames and walls in August heat, and the bees resent every
minute of it. But this sticky brown glue is the hive's own medicine — the
reason a box of forty thousand animals stays cleaner than most kitchens.

We freeze the scrapings, mill them cold, and set them in alcohol for six
weeks, shaken daily. There is no shortcut; the flavonoids leach slowly or
not at all. The tincture on the shelf right now is June's batch. What we
scraped this week becomes the batch you will see in October.

The autumn flowers are opening on the high slope — the dark honey is
coming. One more letter before the season turns.
